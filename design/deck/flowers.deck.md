# Flowers: Nature's Masterpieces

## Introduction

Flowers are the reproductive structures found in flowering plants. They come in a vast array of colors, shapes, and sizes.

---

## Types of Flowers

- **Annuals**: Complete life cycle in one year
- **Perennials**: Return year after year
- **Biennials**: Two-year life cycle

---

## Popular Flowers

1. Roses - Symbol of love
2. Tulips - Spring favorites
3. Sunflowers - Tall and bright
4. Daisies - Simple and cheerful

---

## Flower Anatomy

- Petals: Colored parts
- Sepals: Protective leaves
- Stamens: Male reproductive organs
- Pistils: Female reproductive organs

---

## Fun Facts

- The largest flower is the Rafflesia
- Some flowers bloom only at night
- Flowers use scent to attract pollinators

---

## Conclusion

Flowers bring beauty and joy to our world!
